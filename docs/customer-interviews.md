# Customer interview script

Goal: in 2–3 weeks, find out whether teams will pay to monitor vendors' terms, and whether consumers care enough to sign up.
Rule: listen 80%, talk 20%. Ask about the past, not the future ("what did you do last time", not "would you use").

## Business (privacy, legal, security, procurement): 10 interviews

**Who to find:** privacy counsel, DPOs, GRC or security analysts, procurement leads at companies with 50+ SaaS vendors. LinkedIn, IAPP community, local privacy meetups, your own network.

**Opening (1 min)**
"I'm researching how teams keep track of their vendors' terms and privacy policies. No pitch: I want to learn how you do it today."

**Current process (10 min)**
1. How many software vendors does your company use? Who owns the list?
2. When a vendor changes its terms, privacy policy, DPA or subprocessor list, how do you find out?
3. Tell me about the last time a vendor change mattered. What happened? How did you hear about it?
4. What do you check when reviewing a new vendor's terms? Do you have written rules (AI training, data location, liability, notice periods)?
5. What tools do you use today? (Spreadsheets, OneTrust, Vanta/Drata, Visualping, email alerts, nothing.)

**Pain and cost (5 min)**
6. Roughly how many hours a month go into this? Whose time?
7. What's the worst thing that could happen if you miss a change? Has it happened?
8. If this disappeared tomorrow, what would break?

**Show the demo (5 min)**: open `/teams`, toggle a rule, click a red cell.
9. What's the first thing you'd want to change here?
10. What's missing for you to use this at work?

**Money (3 min)**
11. Who would approve buying something like this? What budget would it come from?
12. If it saved you those hours, what would it be worth per month? (Stay silent after asking.)
13. Would you try a pilot with your real vendor list next month? (Yes with a date = strong signal.)

**Close:** "Who else should I talk to?" Ask for one intro.

## Consumers: 10 short conversations (15 min)

1. When did you last read a privacy policy or terms? Why?
2. Has an app ever done something with your data that surprised you? What did you do?
3. Show the landing page playground and quiz. Watch, don't explain. What did they click first?
4. Which rule matters most to you? Which app would you check first?
5. Would you install an extension that shows a grade on every site? Why or why not?
6. Would you pay $4/month for alerts when apps you use change their terms? What would make it worth it?

## Scoring after each interview

| Signal | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | Recent concrete story, real hours spent | "It would be nice" |
| Current spend | Already pays for a tool or person | Does nothing |
| Next step | Agrees to a pilot with a date or intro | "Send me info" |

**Go:** 3+ businesses agree to a pilot or name a price over $50/month.
**Pivot:** fewer than 3; revisit which segment felt the most pain.
